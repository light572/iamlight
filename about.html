ABOUT

Know thyself.

The Mission

I AM LIGHT exists to explore the deeper dimensions of human existence and to encourage people to discover who they are, why they are here and what they are capable of becoming.

Through revelation, teaching, conversation and spiritual exploration, we examine consciousness, wisdom, purpose, human behaviour and the relationship between the inner world and the world around us.

The Vision

A world of awakened individuals who understand themselves, think deeply, live consciously and recognize the light within.

What We Explore

Consciousness

Awareness, perception, identity and the experience of being human.

Wisdom

Ancient and modern ideas that help us understand ourselves and reality.

Purpose

Questions about meaning, direction, potential and the contribution each person can make.

Spirituality

Exploring faith, scripture, inner transformation and the deeper dimensions of spiritual life.

The Philosophy

We ask questions rather than simply accepting assumptions.

We seek understanding rather than conformity.

We value both ancient wisdom and honest inquiry.

And above all, we encourage the journey of knowing thyself.

I AM LIGHT

The name is an invitation to explore identity at its deepest level — not merely what we have been told we are, but what we can discover through awareness, wisdom and transformation.

Let there be light.
